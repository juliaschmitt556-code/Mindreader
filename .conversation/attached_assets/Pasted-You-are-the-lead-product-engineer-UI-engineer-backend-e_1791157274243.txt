PROJECT NAME:
ReplyMind

PRODUCT:
A mobile-first AI PWA that allows a user to upload a screenshot of a conversation, paste conversation text, or provide a message, and receive context-aware suggested replies.

The core experience should feel like:

UPLOAD SCREENSHOT
        ↓
AI reads conversation
        ↓
Understands context + tone + relationship
        ↓
Generates multiple natural replies
        ↓
User chooses tone/style
        ↓
User copies or regenerates reply

The product must be polished enough to deploy publicly.
